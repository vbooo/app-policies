Measurements on Photos — Privacy

Last updated November 25, 2026 — Android app fr.mesuressurphotos.

Who is responsible for your data?

The publisher and data controller is Fabien Bouvet, acting in his own name. Contact: contact@fabienbouvet.fr. You can also contact us through the app’s feedback form. The app is called “Mesures sur photos” in French.

Your photos and reports stay on your device

Photos you select or take, frames you extract from videos, annotations and report details (name, client, address, date, notes and folders) are processed and stored on your device. We do not receive them. The app does not automatically sync your photos to the cloud.

You choose which files the app can access through Android’s file and photo pickers. The app does not access your contacts or your phone’s GPS location. It has no ads and does not require an account. The free report counter is stored locally.

Sharing, backups and deletion

You choose what to export, where to save it and which app to share it with. Services you choose have their own privacy practices. Generated JPEGs and PDFs do not copy EXIF metadata from the original photos. Opaque covers are flattened into the exported image: the hidden pixels are not included in that image.

A .mspbackup file is different: it contains original photos, any original metadata and editable annotations, including covers that can be removed. The app does not encrypt this archive. Keep it somewhere private.

Reports in the trash can be recovered until you permanently delete them. Clearing the app’s data or uninstalling it removes its private storage. Photos in your gallery, exported files and external backups must be deleted separately. The app disables Android’s automatic backup; phone manufacturers’ transfer tools may work differently.

Subscriptions

Google Play handles payments. RevenueCat verifies purchases, manages Premium and Pro access and helps restore subscriptions. The information needed includes a pseudonymous technical identifier, purchase references and history, the product, subscription dates and status, and technical platform and version information. The app does not send your name, email, photos or reports to RevenueCat. Automatic device identifier collection and optional SDK diagnostics are disabled.

A technical profile may be created even if you have not made a purchase, to check your Premium and Pro access. Network requests also transmit your IP address, which RevenueCat may use to determine your country. This processing provides the subscription you request, helps prevent abuse and supports subscription operations. We do not receive your payment card number. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription; you manage it in Google Play.

Support messages

If you use the feedback form, we receive the category, message, email address if provided, language, app and Android versions, and a technical reference to prevent duplicate submissions. An email is required for questions and optional for bug reports and ideas. We do not retrieve your email from Google Play. Photos and purchase details are not automatically attached.

Supabase hosts the receiving service, OVHcloud delivers the message and Gmail hosts the support inbox. Your IP address is used for network communication and abuse prevention; the support database stores a protected fingerprint rather than the raw IP address. Providers may process technical logs. We use messages to respond, fix problems and improve the app, not for marketing.

How long we keep data

  • Local data: until you permanently delete it or clear the app’s storage.
  • Support queue: message text and email are removed after acceptance by the mail service; receipts, fingerprints and undelivered messages are purged after 30 days under the existing service.
  • Support conversations: while we handle your request, then for up to 12 months after it is resolved.
  • RevenueCat profiles with no purchases: for up to 12 months after the last activity. Subscription data: while your subscription is active, then for up to 24 months after it expires to handle restoration requests and disputes.

The publisher handles deletion from the support inbox and RevenueCat manually. If a legal obligation or an ongoing dispute requires longer retention, only the necessary information is kept separately for that purpose. Google retains its own payment records under its policies. Providers’ technical logs are subject to their security and retention policies, linked below.

Your rights and our service providers

Processing needed to deliver a subscription is based on performing that contract. Support, abuse prevention and service improvement rely on our legitimate interests, depending on the request. Legal obligations may require separate retention. We do not sell your data or use it for targeted advertising.

Where applicable, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your personal data, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Use the contact address above. To locate an anonymous purchase, we may ask for an order reference, never your password or payment card number. You may also complain to the CNIL or your local data protection authority.

Some providers process data outside the European Economic Area. The agreements offered by RevenueCat and Supabase include standard contractual clauses for applicable transfers. You can contact us for information about the safeguards that apply to your data.

See also the privacy information from Google, OVHcloud and GitHub. This page is hosted on GitHub Pages, with no advertising or analytics tools added by us. The host receives technical information needed to serve the page.
