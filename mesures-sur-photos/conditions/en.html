2. Purpose of the app

The app lets you add dimensions, angles, notes and annotations to photos, organize reports and export them.

Dimensions are entered by the user. Calculated angles describe the geometry of the image and may differ from real-world angles because of perspective. The app does not automatically measure the real-world dimensions of an object and is not a certified measuring instrument.

Check measurements and documents before using them for an order, manufacturing or building work. This precaution does not remove statutory warranties or liabilities.
